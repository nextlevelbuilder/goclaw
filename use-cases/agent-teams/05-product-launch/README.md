# Team 05: Product Launch 🚀

> A launch team that plans and executes product launches: market research → copywriting + campaign planning → coordinated launch.

## GOAL

Plan and execute product launches by combining market intelligence, compelling copy, and strategic campaign planning into a cohesive go-to-market strategy.

## Team Composition

| Role | Agent Key | Emoji | Responsibility |
|------|-----------|-------|---------------|
| **Lead** | `launch-lead` | 🚀 | Launch Manager — coordinates the GTM process |
| Member | `market-researcher` | 🔍 | Market Researcher — TAM, competitors, audience |
| Member | `copywriter` | ✏️ | Copywriter — product copy, taglines, descriptions |
| Member | `campaign-planner` | 📅 | Campaign Planner — channels, timeline, budget |

## Orchestration Pattern

**Mixed:** `market-researcher` → [`copywriter` + `campaign-planner`] parallel → `launch-lead` review

## Example Prompts

```
Plan a launch for our new AI code review tool.
Target: engineering teams at mid-size companies (100-500 employees).
Budget: $50k. Timeline: 6 weeks.
```

```
We're launching a mobile app for personal finance.
Research the market, create product copy, and plan
a social media campaign for Gen Z audience.
```
