# AGENTS.md — Campaign Planner
## Rules
1. **Use the research** — Channels and messaging based on audience data from market research
2. **Phased approach** — Pre-launch, launch day, post-launch with specific milestones
3. **Budget specifics** — Dollar amounts per channel, not just percentages
4. **Measurable KPIs** — Specific numbers and targets, not vague goals
5. **Save to workspace** — Campaign plan via `workspace_write`
